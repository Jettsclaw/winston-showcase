The Project Story

How a trophy waterfront home was restored — without risking a single surface.

Salt air and years of canal humidity had dulled the render, glass and stone of one of the Coast's most recognisable waterfront homes. The brief was simple and unforgiving: bring it back, and don't damage anything irreplaceable.

The Problem

Heavy salt and organic staining across render, frameless glass and natural stone — surfaces a high-pressure rig would have marked, hazed or stripped.

The Approach

A surface-by-surface soft-wash plan. Low pressure matched to each material, written and agreed before any water touched the home.

The Result

Restored, not refinished. The home reads as new again — coatings, seals and warranties intact.

The Mechanism — Why Soft Washing

Soft-wash safe, not pressure-blasted.

Most operators run high-pressure rigs — the exact thing that shatters seals on double-glazing, voids roof-tile warranties and strips render coatings. We do the opposite. Low pressure, matched to the surface, so the result is restoration without collateral damage.

High-pressure blasting

Forces water under flooring, hazes glass, cracks seals, lifts render and strips the protective coating off roof tiles. Fast on a driveway — reckless on a finished home.

up to 4,500 PSI

Soft washing

Low pressure with surface-specific solution does the lifting, not brute force. Safe on render, glass, stone, paint and tile — it won't strip the roof's protective coating or void your warranty.

What We Protect

Every service framed by the surface it preserves.

Glass & frameless systems

Window cleaning

The clear glass is the easy part. Protecting frameless and double-glazed systems from seal damage is the part that matters. We clean the glass and respect the build.

02
Render, stone & paint

Soft washing

Our flagship method. Low pressure and surface-specific solution lift years of salt and organic staining from render, stone and painted facades — without marking the finish.

03
Hard surfaces & driveways

Pressure washing

Where pressure belongs — on hard surfaces that can take it. Driveways, paths and pool surrounds brought back to presentation standard, pressure dialled to the substrate.

04

Roof cleaning

The most expensive mistake on a home is a blasted roof. We soft-wash tile and metal so we lift the growth and staining — and leave the protective coating and your warranty intact.

Solar panel cleaning

Salt film and dust quietly cost you output. We clean arrays with low pressure and the right water, protecting the glass and the seals that keep the system performing.
