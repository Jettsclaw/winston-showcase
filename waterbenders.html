Before you let anyone near it

The wrong tool doesn't clean your roof. It cracks it.

01

High pressure cracks tiles

"I don't want anyone cracking my tiles." A blast across a tile or Colorbond roof chips, lifts and shatters the surface you paid a fortune for. There is no undo.

02

It voids your roof warranty

"Will high pressure void my roof warranty?" Most manufacturers will not honour a tile roof that's been high-pressure cleaned. One job, and the cover is gone.

03

It forces water under the tiles

"I heard pressure washing can force water into the roof cavity." Driven under the tiles and into the ceiling, that water shows up as a stain weeks later.

The cost of the wrong contractor dwarfs the price of the clean. So the only question that matters is the method.
