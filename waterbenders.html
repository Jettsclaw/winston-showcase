01 — The real risk

The damage doesn't show up on quote day. It shows up two summers later.

High-pressure cleaning looks dramatic in the moment. Then the seals weep, the render cracks at the joins, and the roof warranty you paid for quietly becomes worthless. By then the operator is three suburbs away and unreachable.

A blasted surface looks clean for a fortnight and compromised for a decade.
  • Shattered window & door sealsForced water past the gasket line — fogging, leaks, full reglazing bills.
  • Voided roof warrantiesManufacturers exclude high-pressure cleaning. One pass can end your cover.
  • Stripped render & paintTexture coat lifted at the edges, exposing substrate to the next storm.
  • Etched stone & timberPermanent furring and pitting on the surfaces that cost the most to replace.
02 — The mechanism

Soft-wash, matched to the surface. Not pressure-blasted.

We don't own a single rig set to maximum and pointed at everything. Each surface gets its own dwell time, dilution and pressure — biology does the lifting, water just rinses.

What most operators do
4,500PSI — one setting, every surface

Force the dirt off

Brute pressure strips the grime and a layer of whatever it's sitting on with it. Fast on the day, expensive the year after.

What Waterbenders do
<500PSI — tuned per surface

Dissolve it, then rinse

Surface-safe solution breaks the bond at the root, then a gentle rinse carries it away. The growth doesn't come straight back, and nothing underneath is touched.
