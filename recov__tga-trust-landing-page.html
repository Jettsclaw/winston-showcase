The mechanism

Absorbed in 15–25 minutes.
No digestive overhead.

Recov's di/tripeptides are absorbed via PEPT1 — a high-capacity, non-saturable peptide transporter in the proximal small intestine. This is the body's fastest route from ingestion to bloodstream.

Free amino acids (BCAAs, amino acid supplements) use separate, saturable transporters that compete with each other. At physiological doses, they hit a transport ceiling. PEPT1 does not.

The result: all 20 amino acids reach your bloodstream intact, simultaneously, in 15–25 minutes. No shake. No digestive cycle. No waiting.
