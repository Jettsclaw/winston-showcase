§ I — The Brief

A prestige listing that needed to look the part

The agent had a problem every Gold Coast luxury agent knows: a Sovereign Islands waterfront property heading to market, but years of salt air, weather exposure, and deferred maintenance had left every exterior surface looking tired. Driveways stained, render dulled, windows hazed, gutters packed.

The vendor needed the property vendor-ready — not in weeks, not with multiple trades to coordinate — but fast, with a single point of contact and no follow-up required.

§ II — Scope of Works

One call. Every surface. 762 m².

Waterbenders deployed a full property rejuvenation across every exterior surface of 57 Royal Albert Crescent. No subcontractors, no coordination headaches for the agent, no callbacks.

  • Pressure Cleaning Driveways, paths, pool surrounds, retaining walls — all hardscapes restored.
  • House Washing Full render wash. Salt residue, mould, and weathering removed from all elevations.
  • Window Cleaning Every window and glass surface, inside and out — streak-free for listing photography.
  • Gutters Cleared, flushed, and inspected. Presentation-ready from every angle, including aerial drone shots.
