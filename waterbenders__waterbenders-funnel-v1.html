The Reality

Your Property Is Silently Deteriorating

Salt air. Queensland sun. Tropical humidity. Every prestige property on the Gold Coast is under constant assault. The mould you can see is only the beginning. The damage you can't see is what costs real money.

Render Degradation

Algae and salt deposits eat into rendered surfaces, turning a $50K facade into a $50K liability. What starts as a green tinge becomes structural if left.

Solar Output Loss

Dirty solar panels lose up to 25% efficiency. On a prestige property with a large array, that's thousands in lost generation every year you don't clean them.
