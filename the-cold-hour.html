The Hour

What your first hour actually feels like — minute by minute.

Min 00 · Arrive

Tea, not a turnstile

You arrive to a quiet room and a warm drink. Your coach introduces themselves, learns your name, and tells you exactly what the next sixty minutes hold. No experience needed. Nothing to prove yet.

Min 10 · Sauna

Choose your bench

Heat first. You settle in, breathe, and let the noise of the day come down. This is the part that makes the cold worth it.

Min 30 · Plunge

You're always in charge of how deep you go

Sub-5°C. Your coach counts you in and holds you through every breath. There is no dunking, no shoving, no surprise. "As deep as you want" is the rule, not the upsell.

Min 45 · Return

The warm bridge

Out of the cold and into red-light. The body comes back online. This is where the calm arrives — the thing the home dunk never gave you because you skipped it.

Min 55 · Reset

Walk out a different person than walked in

Five minutes to sit in it. Then back to your day — clear, level, and quietly proud you did the hard thing on purpose.
